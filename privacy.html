Yommo! Privacy Policy

Effective: August 9, 2026 · Applies to the Yommo! app and yommo.app

What Yommo! is

Yommo! is a private family calendar. Your family's information is visible only to the family members you invite — there is no public feed, no strangers, and no advertising.

Information we collect

  • Account: your name, email address, and a password (stored securely by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see your password).
  • Family content: events, tasks, family member names and birthdays, photos and drawings you choose to share, and team schedules. This content is stored so your family can see it.
  • Settings: language (English/Spanish) and reminder preferences.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing history, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How your information is used

  • To show your family's shared calendar, board, tasks, and photos to your family members.
  • To send email reminders about upcoming events, if enabled — you can turn these off in Settings.
  • If you use the schedule scanner, the image you upload is processed by an AI service to read the dates, then discarded by that service; only the resulting events are saved.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never share family content with anyone outside your family.
  • We never show ads.

Children

Yommo! is a family tool. Children's profiles are created and managed by a parent or guardian. A child account can only join a family with an invite code from their family, and children's information is visible only inside that family.

Where data lives

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) on Google's secure infrastructure. Reminder emails are sent via Resend from reminders@yommo.app.

Deleting your data

You can delete events, photos, tasks, and family members inside the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, email reminders@yommo.app from your account email with the subject "Delete my account" and we will remove it within 30 days.

Contact

Questions? Email reminders@yommo.app.

Política de privacidad (resumen en español)
